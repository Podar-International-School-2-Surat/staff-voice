# Listening Circles — External Facilitator Guide

For the **external trainer** who runs the Principal-blind listening circles. These circles are source 2 of the three-source check (survey → circles → behavioural data) used with the Leadership 360 survey.

## 1. Ground rules (agree in writing before the first circle)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Principal absent** | The Principal, Vice Principal and coordinators of the group being heard do not attend, enter, or wait outside. |
| **Themes only** | The facilitator reports themes with frequency ("most / several / a few"). No names, no quotes that identify a person, class, or incident. |
| **Quote test** | A verbatim quote goes into the report only if 3+ people could have said it. |
| **No notes leave the room** | Handwritten notes are destroyed after the report is signed off; no audio recording. |
| **Principal does not ask who said what** | Written into the engagement letter. A breach ends the exercise. |
| **Escalation exception** | Safety, harassment, or legal matters are *not* kept confidential. The facilitator states this up front and, if one arises, tells the speaker what will happen next. |

## 2. Set-up

- **Groups of 6–8**, split by peer level: Primary / Middle / Secondary / Coordinators & HODs / Non-teaching. Never mix a coordinator with their own team.
- **45 minutes**, neutral room, scheduled in school time (not after hours).
- Invitation sent by the facilitator, not the Principal. Attendance is voluntary.
- Run circles in the **same fortnight as the survey** so the two can be compared.

## 3. 45-minute run sheet

| Min | Step | Script / method |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | **Safety opening** | State the six ground rules. "Nothing said here is attributed. I will report patterns only." |
| 5–10 | **Icebreaker: One-word weather** | Each person gives one word for "leadership at this school this term." Write words on the board; no explanations yet. |
| 10–25 | **Start / Stop / Continue** | Silent writing (3 min, sticky notes: one per note), then cluster on the wall. Prompt: *"What should school leadership start, stop, continue doing?"* |
| 25–35 | **Case-story round** | "Tell me about a time a decision reached you well — and a time it didn't." Ask for the *situation*, not the person. |
| 35–42 | **Dot vote** | Each person gets 3 dots to mark the themes that matter most. |
| 42–45 | **Close** | Read back the top 3 themes. Ask: "Is this a fair summary? Anything missing that is safe to add?" Remind them of the feedback-loop date. |

**Probing questions that get past polite answers:**
- "What do people say about this in the staffroom that they wouldn't say in a meeting?"
- "If a new teacher joined tomorrow, what would you quietly warn them about?"
- "What happens when someone disagrees with a decision — what have you *seen*?"
- "What would make you leave? What keeps you here?"
- "On a 1–10, how safe is it to disagree with leadership? What makes it that number?"

**Facilitator moves:** thank hard comments neutrally ("that's helpful"), never defend leadership, ask "can you give an example?", and name when only one voice dominates ("let's hear from someone who hasn't spoken yet").

## 4. Report to the Principal (template)

Use the same six dimensions as the survey so the dashboard's triangulation worksheet can use it directly.

```
LISTENING CIRCLE REPORT — Round: ______  Date: ______
Circles held: __ | Participants: __ (groups: ____________)
Facilitator: ______________  Signed: ______

For each dimension, give: Signal (Positive / Mixed / Negative), strength of agreement
(most / several / a few), a one-line theme, and a safe-to-quote line (only if 3+ could have said it).

1. Vision & Direction            Signal: ____  Agreement: ____  Theme: ______________
2. Instructional Leadership      Signal: ____  Agreement: ____  Theme: ______________
3. Fairness & Consistency        Signal: ____  Agreement: ____  Theme: ______________
4. Approachability & Voice       Signal: ____  Agreement: ____  Theme: ______________
5. Recognition & Development     Signal: ____  Agreement: ____  Theme: ______________
6. Systems & Communication       Signal: ____  Agreement: ____  Theme: ______________

Top 3 "Start / Stop / Continue" themes by dot vote:
  START: ______  STOP: ______  CONTINUE: ______

Safety level observed (how freely did people speak?): 1–5 ____  Evidence: ____________
Anything urgent (safety / harassment / legal): Yes / No — handled as: ____________
```

The **"safety level observed"** line matters: if the circle itself was guarded, treat positive signals from that group with caution.

## 5. After the circles — the Principal's part

1. Read the report **without asking who said anything**.
2. Within **30 days**, share a "You said → We did" note with all staff: three things heard, two actions taken, one thing not changing and why.
3. Enter the facilitator's signals into the **triangulation worksheet** in `analysis/leadership-dashboard.html`.
4. Act first where survey + circles + behavioural data **all agree**. Investigate where they conflict.

## 6. Engagement letter checklist

- [ ] Purpose: the Principal's own leadership growth, not staff appraisal.
- [ ] Facilitator independence (not employed by or reporting to the school).
- [ ] The six ground rules in section 1, signed by the Principal and the facilitator.
- [ ] Data handling: notes destroyed, report holds themes only.
- [ ] Dates: circles, report delivery, staff feedback-loop briefing.
