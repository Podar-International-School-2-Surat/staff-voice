Leadership Listening

An anonymous, twice-a-year check on how school leadership is experienced by the people who work here. About 6 minutes. No names, no logins.

Why now: a Principal who is only ever told "everything is fine" cannot improve. This round is for the Principal's own growth — it is not an appraisal of any staff member and never will be used as one.

How your answers are protected: no name, email or device ID is collected; only the date (not time) is stored; results are only ever shown for groups of 5 or more; written comments are shown in random order with nothing attached.

What happens next: within 30 days staff will see a "You said → We did" note on what was heard and what is changing.

Please be specific. One concrete example is worth more than ten ticks. If a comment names a person or incident, you are choosing to be identifiable — describe the pattern instead.
